DATA & METHOD

A clear starting point.

This release adds an EMODnet mean-depth raster to geographic context and a generated reference grid. It contains no suitability scores or farm proposals.

Navigation exclusions

OpenStreetMap ferry, waterway=fairway and recommended-track lines from the downloaded 2 November 2026 extracts, including Egholmfærgen. Paddling routes (boat=discouraged, canoe=yes) are separately labelled. No claim is made that all vessel movements or official shipping lanes are represented. Individual feature links and tags are retained in the GeoJSON. User-selected corridors use the adjustable distance on each side of the centreline (100 m initially). These are analysis exclusions, not legal safety distances. A local planar metric approximation at 57.07° N is used consistently for display and point classification. Inactive points retain their IDs and exclusion reason, but their environmental values are blank in live CSV exports; original source files and pre-baked downloads remain unfiltered.

Wind

ERA5 via Open-Meteo Historical Weather API, hourly 10 m wind speed and meteorological direction for all of 2025. Six surrounding 0.25° source nodes cover the study area. Monthly speed is the arithmetic mean of hourly scalar speeds. Monthly u/v are averaged separately after converting FROM bearings: u = −speed × sin(direction), v = −speed × cos(direction). Display arrows show TOWARDS bearing from the mean vector; the inspector also gives FROM bearing. These are not gusts or maximum speeds. Monthly scalars and components are bilinearly interpolated separately; no angles are averaged. Net-vector directions below 0.1 m/s are hidden. Colour shows mean speed, not net magnitude. Native resolution is approximately 25 km, so site-scale variation is not resolved. Repeated arrow density and grid sampling do not increase source resolution. ERA5 / Copernicus and Open-Meteo attribution, CC BY 4.0 for Open-Meteo data.

Basemap

Map tiles: © OpenStreetMap contributors. Geographic context is community-maintained and requires an internet connection. The imported coastline curves use OpenStreetMap coastline ways. These are mapped shorelines, not a site survey or a live tidal boundary.

Coastline and water mask

© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0. Retrieved 2 November 2026 through the OSM API. Coastline ways are assembled into polygons using their direction (land on the left) and closed at the study-window boundary. Egholm, neighbouring islands and mainland are excluded; inland lakes are not included in this coastal-water mask. Points on the polygon boundary are excluded. Coastline visibility does not switch off clipping.

Reference grid

Grid coordinates use WGS 84 / UTM zone 32N (EPSG:32632), in metres. Points are transformed to longitude and latitude for display. IDs encode spacing, easting and northing. Original mode reports containing source cells. Estimated modes sample bilinearly from up to four surrounding source-cell centres onto UTM grid nodes. Invalid, above-datum and on-land source neighbours are excluded; remaining weights are normalised. No value is returned if no valid weighted neighbour exists. Grid spacing does not increase source accuracy.

Study window

9.700–9.930° E, 57.015–57.130° N. Expanded north and west from Egholm. This provisional rectangular extent includes Egholm and nearby water and mainland. It is a working extent, not an administrative boundary.

Water clarity: surface turbidity

Copernicus Marine, Sentinel-2 MSI HR-OC Baltic L4 monthly mosaic, dataset cmems_obs-oc_bal_bgc-tur-spm-chl_nrt_l4-hr-mosaic_P1M-m_202511. Retrieved 2 November 2026. Archived data also use dataset cmems_obs_oc_bal_bgc_transp_nrt_l4-hr_P1M-m_202105, tile 32V. Each snapshot retains its own source coordinate grid; no resampling is used to align archive and mosaic grids. Values are satellite-derived monthly turbidity estimates in FNU, at approximately 100 m source resolution. They represent surface optical conditions, not bottom-water observations, Secchi depth, or measured light at the seabed.

Eleven calendar months have usable snapshots. January, February and October use 2025 legacy tiles; March–September use 2026 mosaic data; November uses 2025 mosaic data. Selection uses the most recent usable snapshot among checked 2024–2026 sources. No values are blended between years or borrowed from a different calendar month. The selector shows month names; the inspector and downloads retain actual observation dates. December 2024 and December 2025 original files contain no valid turbidity values in the study window. December 2026 is in the future at retrieval, so December remains explicitly unavailable. This is a downloaded snapshot, not a live feed. The count field is the number of daily observations contributing to each monthly pixel, not the number of field samples. Cloud, shore and processing masks can leave gaps. Counts are a coverage indicator, not an accuracy guarantee.

We retain the original monthly values and counts, reject negative/non-finite values and cells with no observations, and mask to coastal water. Smooth mode uses bilinear interpolation from up to four surrounding valid source centres, renormalising available weights. The containing source cell must also be valid and meet the observation filter: missing source cells are never filled. Values are sampled at the selected UTM water-grid points, then blended between neighbouring grid nodes for the smooth surface. The same source-validity gate is applied during rendering. Original mode retains source values. Interpolated values and the minimum daily count among contributing cells are identified separately; observation counts are not interpolated. No temporal filling is performed. All twelve month records are attached to each grid point; December remains null. The CSV includes actual source dates, original cell values, estimated values and support counts. The pre-baked 100 m CSV uses the one-observation filter; the live export uses the selected spacing and filter. Changing grid density does not create additional observations. The legend is a visual scale, not an ecological suitability threshold.

Chlorophyll-a

Copernicus Marine Sentinel-2 Baltic HR-OC monthly CHL estimates, in mg/m³. The same calendar-month selection as turbidity is retained: January, February and October 2025 legacy geophy tiles; March–September 2026 and November 2025 mosaics. December has no supported values. These snapshots are not a single-year series or a climatology. Chlorophyll-a is an indicator of phytoplankton abundance, not a direct measure of mussel food quality or eelgrass health. The same source-validity checks, observation-count filter, water clipping and bilinear grid sampling apply. Original data and estimated values remain separate.

Surface salinity

DMI DKSS Limfjord model, parameter salinity, g/kg. A timestamped forecast snapshot, not a monthly observation. The downloaded file retains the model run, valid time, source URL and retrieval date. Source grid spacing is approximately 185 m. The model ends near 57.109° N; water outside its coverage remains blank. Source model land cells and unsupported coastal cells remain missing. Salinity uses model support rather than satellite observation counts; the observation-count filter is disabled. Interpolation is spatial only and does not create extra measurements. This is a stored snapshot, not an automatically refreshed feed.

DMI model documentation ↗ · DMI terms of use ↗

Temperature colours

Automatic scaling uses the minimum and maximum supported values on the selected water grid for the selected month and display mode. Each month can have a different legend: equal colours across months do not imply equal temperatures. Fixed scaling uses −2 to 25°C for comparisons. Custom bounds are saved separately for each temperature dataset and month in this browser. Values beyond the bounds use the endpoint colours; data and exports are unchanged. The green–yellow–red ramp indicates relative temperature, not ecological suitability. Small differences may appear strong under a stretched scale; this does not increase the model resolution.

Monthly temperature: regional context

Copernicus Baltic Sea Physics Reanalysis, cmems_mod_bal_phy_my_P1M-m_202303, potential temperature thetao at approximately 0.5 m depth. All twelve calendar months use 2025 monthly means. Native spacing is about 1.85 km; this coarse model does not resolve Egholm channels or site-scale thermal differences. This is seasonal context, not a locally validated temperature map. Native model wet cells are retained; display and sampling are clipped to the finer coastal water polygon. Original source mode preserves all missing cells. With coastal estimates enabled, missing monthly-temperature cells are estimated from up to four valid source centres within 2.5 km using inverse-distance-squared weighting. Estimates are sampled only on the water grid and the display is clipped to the detailed coastline. This geometric method does not model flow connectivity around islands. Original values remain null; inspector and CSV identify coastal estimates and nearest-source distance. More distant gaps remain empty. Interpolation onto a 100 m grid does not create 100 m model accuracy. No temporal interpolation or mixing of years is used. The detailed DMI temperature forecast remains a separate option.

Monthly net currents: regional context

Copernicus Baltic Sea Physics Reanalysis, cmems_mod_bal_phy_my_P1M-m_202303: January–December 2025, uo eastward and vo northward monthly mean velocity at about 0.5 m depth, native spacing about 1.85 km. Components are interpolated before calculating net magnitude and direction. The magnitude of a monthly mean vector is NOT mean current speed: opposing flows cancel. It cannot describe peak currents, tidal reversals or local channel flow. Optional coastal estimates use inverse-distance-squared weighting of up to four valid source centres within 2.5 km, separately for eastward and northward components, before deriving net magnitude and bearing. Existing source-supported values are unchanged. Estimates are clipped to the water polygon; this does not simulate flow around islands or enforce shoreline flow conditions. Inspector and exports flag coastal estimates and source distance; original source values remain null. Switch off coastal estimates or select Original source cells to restore source gaps. Arrows below 0.0001 m/s are hidden for display; this threshold and four decimal places do not imply model accuracy. Each month’s components, net magnitude, direction and source date are exported on the water grid. Use this as exploratory regional context only. The detailed DMI forecast remains separate.

Copernicus monthly current source ↗

Water temperature and currents

DMI DKSS Limfjord surface model parameters water-temperature (°C), current-u and current-v (m/s). These use the same model run and valid time as the salinity snapshot. They are forecasts, not monthly averages or seabed measurements. Current u is eastward and v northward. We interpolate the components onto the water grid, then calculate speed as √(u² + v²) and the direction towards which water moves, clockwise from true north. The smooth surface blends grid components before deriving speed; angles are never averaged directly. Arrows have fixed length and are thinned for readability; all supported water-grid points retain numerical values. Directions below 0.01 m/s are omitted as near-calm. Exports include estimated u/v, speed, direction and original source speed/direction. Coverage gaps remain empty.

Bathymetry

Source: EMODnet Bathymetry WCS, coverage emodnet:mean, retrieved 2 November 2026. This is a service snapshot; retrieval date is not survey date. The service describes a nominal 1/16 arc-minute source grid.

Downloaded extent: 9.66–10.00° E, 56.98–57.16° N; 326 × 173 cells, approximately 63 × 116 metres here. WCS values use negative elevation below the reference datum. We display their magnitude as positive depth below LAT, and render only below-datum cells. This threshold is not a surveyed coastline or water mask: low-lying land and shoreline cells require checking.

Original mode preserves raster cells. Grid-cell mode colours the chosen UTM grid cells using estimated node values. Smooth mode bilinearly blends those grid-node estimates; near the shoreline it normalises the weights of available water nodes. All views are clipped to the coastal water polygon. Missing source support remains a gap. Smooth rendering is capped at 720 display pixels per side, independently of numerical sampling. Near-shore estimates need particular caution: this interpolation does not model tidal changes or hydrodynamic connectivity. Click values are rounded to 0.1 m for readability, not a claim of accuracy. Colour edits affect visualisation only; depths are unchanged. The final colour extends beyond the final legend break.

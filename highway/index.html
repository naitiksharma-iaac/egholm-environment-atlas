ABOUT THE EXPERIMENT

One gesture.
Many possible paths.

The bar follows your pointer with a little inertia. Its tracing points leave curves behind as it moves and rotates. Try reversing direction, drawing a small circle, or turning the mouse wheel during a stroke.

This is an independent reconstruction of the visible interaction in Jeune Homme’s reel. It is not the original tool or a verified copy of its algorithm.

Your drawing stays in this browser. Use SVG or PNG to keep a copy. Changing settings affects the next stroke; undo restores cleared drawings too.

ABOUT THE EXPERIMENT

One gesture.
Many possible paths.

Two independent trails follow your pointer with different amounts of inertia. Steady movement brings them together; changes of direction separate them and can curl them into loops. Slow, gentle curves widen into pockets.

The pocket is a 2D footprint for exploring your volière idea. This version is entirely a drawing study. Crossings are visual joins, not an engineered route network.

An independent experiment inspired by Jeune Homme’s reel, using our own motion rules. The example supplies only pointer movement to the same engine you use.

Your drawing stays in this browser. Earlier drawings are retained. SVG or PNG keeps a copy; clear can be undone. Optional adjustments affect new strokes.

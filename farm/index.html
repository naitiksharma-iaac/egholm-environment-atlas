What these checks do not prove

The load model is a fixed-cylinder approximation. Anchors, fatigue, stability, storms outside the envelope, biology, food supply and benthic effects need separate validation. A geometry or load pass is not permission to install.

How the workbench computes

Geometry checks use the full mooring envelope against land, Natura and route buffers. Depth checks require supporting 100 m grid cells under the cultivation body; missing support remains unknown. Regional and dated model values are not local measurements. The mechanics panel uses your explicit assumptions and a simplified wave/current load model.

Reference models: Holbach et al. and Landmann et al.. See the design-test report. Data: DMI, Copernicus Marine, EMODnet, ICES, ERA5/Open-Meteo, Danmarks Miljøportal, Danish terrain data and © OpenStreetMap contributors. This is an architectural research tool.
